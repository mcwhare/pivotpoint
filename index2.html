FAQ

Common questions

What will I learn in these courses?
Our curriculum covers the full career preparation journey — from self-assessment and industry research to crafting compelling application documents, acing interviews, and negotiating offers. Content is tailored to university students entering competitive industries like finance, consulting, marketing, and tech.
How is the course structured?
Courses are broken into short, focused modules you can complete in your own time. Each module builds on the last, and you can revisit any lesson whenever you need to. Live elements like mentorship sessions and community Q&As complement the self-paced content.
Can I contact the instructor directly?
Yes — monthly mentorship sessions give you direct access to Keith and the PivotPoint team. You can also reach out through community channels between sessions for quick questions or document reviews.
Will this course help me get an internship or job?
That's the goal. Our content is built entirely around practical outcomes — landing real roles, not just collecting knowledge. Students who engage fully with mentorship and apply the frameworks consistently see strong results in competitive application cycles.
